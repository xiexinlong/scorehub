1. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

1.1 Account Registration and Login

When you register and log in to a ScoreHub account, you must complete a registration process according to our instructions. During this process, you will need to provide your mobile phone number for registration, login, and verification code delivery.

Your phone number and verification code matching result are considered personal information. We will protect such information in accordance with applicable law. We collect this information based on legal and regulatory requirements. If you refuse to provide it, you may not be able to register an account or use some ScoreHub features.

1.2 Payment Processing

When you subscribe to a paid membership or purchase other value-added services on the ScoreHub Platform, we may collect information related to the transaction, including the nickname and avatar displayed by your third-party payment account, but we will not collect your full account credentials. We may also collect order-related information such as transaction amount, payment time, order number, order status, payment method, and payment status. We collect this information to help you complete transactions smoothly and to help protect transaction security. If you refuse to provide the information described in this section, you may not be able to use the related payment function.

1.3 Feedback and Support

When you submit feedback or make an inquiry, we may need to collect your name, mobile phone number, email address, or third-party platform account information so that we can contact you or help resolve your issue. If you refuse to provide this information, we may not be able to respond to your feedback or inquiry in a timely manner.

1.4 Push Notifications

When you use our services, we may use your mobile phone number to send you SMS messages, news, or other notices. If you refuse to provide this information, you may not receive our push notifications, but this will not affect your ability to use other ScoreHub services.

1.5 Exceptions to Consent

We may collect and use certain personal information without your consent in the following situations, as permitted by applicable law:

  1. Where directly related to national security or national defense security;
  2. Where directly related to public safety, public health, or major public interests;
  3. Where directly related to criminal investigation, prosecution, trial, or enforcement of judgments;
  4. Where necessary to protect the life, property, or other major lawful rights and interests of you or another person, and consent is difficult to obtain in a timely manner;
  5. Where the personal information has been publicly disclosed by you;
  6. Where the personal information is collected from lawfully and publicly disclosed information, such as lawful news reports or public government information;
  7. Where necessary to enter into or perform a contract at your request;
  8. Other circumstances permitted by applicable law.

2. How We Store and Protect Your Personal Information

2.1 Retention

Unless otherwise required by law or agreed by both parties, we retain your personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to achieve the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. When you close your account, we will delete your personal information in accordance with applicable law. Information retained beyond the necessary period will be deleted or anonymized as soon as reasonably practicable. If applicable law requires us to retain certain information, we will no longer use it for any other purpose.

We may adjust the retention period in the following circumstances:

  1. To comply with applicable laws and regulations or where we reasonably believe compliance is required;
  2. To comply with court judgments, orders, or other legal procedures;
  3. To comply with requests from government authorities or legally authorized organizations;
  4. To perform relevant service agreements or this Privacy Policy, protect public interest, or protect the personal or property safety and other lawful rights and interests of our customers, us, our affiliates, other users, or employees.

2.2 Storage Location

Your personal information is stored within the United States or other locations where our service providers operate, unless otherwise required by law or separately authorized by you. Where transfer is required, we will require the recipient to process personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and other applicable security and confidentiality obligations.

2.3 Service Termination

If our operations are terminated, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you at least 30 days in advance and will delete or anonymize your personal information after termination, unless retention is required by law. We will retain order records for three years where required by law.

2.4 Information Security

To protect the security of your information, we use reasonable physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards designed to prevent leakage, damage, or loss. These measures may include SSL encrypted transmission, encrypted storage, and access controls in our data centers. Employees who may access your information are subject to strict management measures, including role-based access control, confidentiality obligations, and operational monitoring.

If your parent or guardian does not agree to your use of our services or to your providing information under this Privacy Policy, you should stop using our services immediately and notify us promptly. If we confirm such a situation, we also have the right to stop providing services to you immediately.

Where personal information of minors is collected with parental or legal guardian consent, we will use or publicly disclose such information only where permitted by law, expressly consented to by the parent or guardian, or necessary to protect the minor.

If you are the parent or guardian of a minor and have questions about the minor's use of our services or the information provided to us, please contact us in a timely manner. We will protect the confidentiality and security of minor users' information in accordance with applicable law and this Privacy Policy. If we discover that we have collected a minor's personal information without first obtaining verifiable parental or legal guardian consent, we will promptly delete the relevant data.
